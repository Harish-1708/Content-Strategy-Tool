## Origin / founder

**Founder:** Kyle Bergman, Founder & Chief Swoverall Officer of The Great Fantastic.
**Background (verified):** Drexel University, bachelor's in Psychology, 2012; played on the men's lacrosse team. First entrepreneurial venture was a club lacrosse programme and tournament run with a friend during his first Drexel co-op; later co-ops with a fashion photography company and a small tomato sauce company. His mother suggested retail; he joined the Bloomingdale's Executive Training programme and worked there 4 years (assistant buyer, luggage; senior assistant, women's fragrances; associate merchandise planner, jewelry). Joined Birchbox in May 2016 as Men's Grooming Buyer (2 years). Began a part-time MBA at NYU Stern while at Birchbox. Also a member of the Israel Men's National Lacrosse team, an Orangetheory Fitness trainer, and played on three world-championship teams.
- Source: https://www.thetriangle.org/article/drexel-grad-kyle-bergmans-swoveralls-on-shark-tank (Drexel Triangle, 2019-05-03); https://www.the360mag.com/on-shark-tank-swoveralls-worlds-comfiest-apparel/ (360 Magazine, 2019-05-21)

**The idea (founder's own words):** "At first, I was really just trying to solve a personal problem - Sweatpant overalls didn't exist, and I wanted a pair. I've always loved overalls for reasons I can't really explain."
- Source: https://www.starterstory.com/stories/sweatpants-overalls-how-i-turned-a-silly-idea-into-a-viral-brand (Starter Story, founder interview, published 2019; updated 2025-04-10)

**The trigger:** early 2016, a friend sent him a BuzzFeed article about novelty sweatpant overalls that looked like denim. The article showed only women's versions; he wanted a pair for himself. No normal sweatpant overalls existed online or in stores. He found a factory drawing of men's sweatpant overalls on Alibaba (minimum order 300 units), negotiated a single sample, and received it.
- Source: Starter Story interview above; https://sidehustleschool.com/episode/3042/ (Side Hustle School, episode 3042, 2025-04-30)

**The demand check:** in March 2017 he used Google's Keyword Planner and found 100–1,000 people a month searching for "sweatpant overalls". (A later brand film rounds this to "about 500 people per month".)
- Source: Starter Story interview above; https://www.instagram.com/reel/DaVwb0dvCRT/ (brand film, @swoveralls_, posted 2026-07-03)

**The company name:** The Great Fantastic, LLC, incorporated March 2017. "Taking a trip into the great fantastic is something my Mom would say when referring to risk-taking and adventuring into the unknown."
- Source: Starter Story interview above; https://swoveralls.com/pages/about

**Funding the first order:** he had taken out more student loan money than needed for his first MBA term and received a ~$10,000 disbursement. "I decided to use this money to kickstart my sweatpant overalls project, and used most of it to pay for my first order from overseas." He sent $10,000 to a supplier in China he had never met in person, only spoken to through Alibaba messaging. First order: 500 pairs (250 grey, 250 navy). No patents ("patenting apparel designs is like the wild west"); he did trademark the term "Swoveralls".
- Source: Starter Story interview above

**Mission (brand's own words):** "Our mission is to create the world's comfiest apparel using sustainable resources and ethical manufacturing processes." The brand's tagline, used in the 2026 brand film: "Free the Waistband" — "to make people more comfortable both literally and figuratively."
- Source: https://swoveralls.com/pages/about; https://www.the360mag.com/on-shark-tank-swoveralls-worlds-comfiest-apparel/ (2019-05-21); Instagram brand film 2026-07-03 (link above)

**Product, as the brand describes it:** sweatpants + overalls. Six pockets (including a hidden zip pocket in the bib), adjustable straps with metal buckles, jogger cuffs, zipper fly, no waistband. French terry, cotton-rich with a touch of stretch; GOTS-certified organic cotton. Gender neutral, sizes XXS–4XL (some styles to 5XL). Made in Los Angeles. Retail $95 at Shark Tank time (2019).
- Source: https://swoveralls.com/pages/meet-swoveralls; https://swoveralls.com/pages/about; 360 Magazine 2019-05-21

**Giveback:** proud partner/member of 1% for the Planet, Urban Pathways, and the Israel Lacrosse Association. A percentage of net profits from the Swoveralls 2.0 crowdfunding was donated to Urban Pathways and the Israel Lacrosse Association.
- Source: 360 Magazine 2019-05-21; https://www.prweb.com/releases/Sweatpant_overalls_Hybrid_Swoveralls_2_0_Nears_the_End_of_its_Successful_Kickstarter_Campaign/prweb15940523.htm (PRWeb, 2018-07-02)

**Founder today (side ventures):** partner and COO at Pinkberg (marketing strategy company) since 2023; co-founder and coach of Keep The Pace (exercise programmes) since 2021. In March 2020 he started a separate business, Sweat from Home, with two co-founders. The brand film (July 2026) shows his twin toddlers Max and Bernie.
- Source: https://www.women.com/1572934/what-went-down-swoveralls-after-shark-tank/ (updated 2025-01-31); https://www.starterstory.com/stories/how-we-increased-our-sales-155-last-year (Starter Story follow-up, 2021-05-26); Instagram brand film 2026-07-03

## Milestones

- **Early 2016** — BuzzFeed article sparks the idea; friend sends it to Bergman while he is a merchandise planner at Bloomingdale's. (Starter Story)
- **March 2017** — The Great Fantastic, LLC incorporated. Google Keyword Planner shows 100–1,000 monthly searches for "sweatpant overalls". (Starter Story)
- **September 2017** — first sales launch (Amazon + direct-to-consumer). "I sold my first pair about 18 months ago" (Starter Story, April 2019) points to ~Oct 2017; the Shark Tank pitch says "launched in September". Exact day not published.
- **First year** — "a little over $65k in sales between Amazon and my own site" (Starter Story, founder). Side Hustle School: "over $65,000 in sales in his first year, resulting in $24,000 in profit." (Note: Washington Square News says "$230,000 in the first year" — that conflicts; see Gaps. The $230,000 figure is consistently reported as total sales by pitch time, April 2019.)
- **2017–2018** — first press: Fatherly, then Business Insider (Starter Story). Thrillist feature via a lacrosse-network connection; picked up by local news and blogs worldwide (Side Hustle School).
- **June–July 2018** — Swoveralls 2.0 crowdfunding: Kickstarter raised ~$75,000 (goal $10,000, beaten "in mere hours" / within the first 24 hours), Indiegogo ~$33,000, ~$108,000 total. 600+ backers. Production moved from overseas to Los Angeles. A Facebook video made with Margle Media went viral (9M+ views) via the "22 words" page. PRWeb release 2018-07-02 notes attention from The Today Show and UNILAD.
  - Source: Starter Story; https://www.prweb.com/releases/Sweatpant_overalls_Hybrid_Swoveralls_2_0_Nears_the_End_of_its_Successful_Kickstarter_Campaign/prweb15940523.htm (2018-07-02); Shark Tank Recap
- **July 2018** — introduced to a Shark Tank casting producer through a Birchbox connection. (Triangle)
- **September 2018** — flew to California to film Shark Tank. (Triangle)
- **21 April 2019** — Shark Tank Season 10 Episode 20 airs on ABC. Ask $150,000 for 15%; no deal. ~13,000 website visits the night it aired; 60,000+ in the week after. 1,500% sales spike reported (360 Mag, 2019-05-21). Backorder 6–8 weeks. New products: Swovie Shorts (winner), Kids Swoveralls, limited editions (tie-dye, Light Heather Grey, Pink for a breast cancer awareness foundation, NASA collaboration).
