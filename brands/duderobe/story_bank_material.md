**Headline:** DudeRobe is a men's hooded bathrobe/loungewear brand founded by Howie Busch (Long Island, New York). Kickstarter June 2017 ($67,498), Shark Tank Season 9, Episode 16 (aired 14 Jan 2018, no deal). It is a **separate company from DUDE Products / Dude Wipes** — different founders, different city, different product. See "DUDE Wipes connection" below.

---

## Origin / founder

**Founder:** Howie Busch, from Long Island, New York. Former attorney and former sports agent (Boardroom calls him a "former NFL agent"); inventor before DudeRobe — he created the On-Hand Pillow and the World's Smallest Travel Pillow, sold in airports worldwide. Former coach for inventRight. (Sources: Gazette Review, 4 Aug 2018; Boardroom, 15 Dec 2021; Jon Dwoskin interview, 16 Apr 2021; inventRight TV podcast.)

**The idea (founder's own account, brand's Our Story page):**
> "It was 2016. I'm in the shower… Then I turned around and saw my hoodie hanging on the hook next to my towel. That was it. What if a robe looked and felt like a hoodie, but actually dried you off?"
> — duderobe.com, Our Story page (undated, live Nov 2026)

He was never a "robe guy" but noticed his wife wore her robe every day and looked comfortable. He drove to Walmart, bought a stack of towels, and took them to his tailor José: *"José, can you make a hoodie out of these towels?"* One week later he had a prototype — hooded robe, tapered sleeves, permanently attached belt. (Same source.)

**Earlier telling (Gazette Review, 4 Aug 2018):** Busch saw his wife's bathrobe hanging next to a hoodie on a hook, drove to Walmart, bought six towels, and worked with his tailor on several prototypes over the next week (robe, sweatshirt, shorts).

**Work began 2015:** PRWeb launch release (19 Jun 2017) says "The team has been working hard since 2015… they have developed samples and is in negotiations with manufacturers."

**The product:** a hooded bathrobe that is sweatshirt material outside, terry towel inside — plus matching shorts, pants, hoodies. Features the brand stresses: permanently attached ("Never-Lost") belt, tapered/cuffed sleeves, deep pockets, hidden stash pocket. Current list price $128 (checked 1 Nov 2026, Yawnder); often sold at $89.60 (30% off) on duderobe.com.

**Founder quotes (PRWeb, 19 Jun 2017):**
> "We re-designed and re-engineered the robe into something today's guys will actually want to wear — something that's absorbent and also looks cool… You can wear it for lounging, but also when you walk the dog or get your morning coffee."
> "There are no good options for men's robes on the market today. The only alternative is the boring and oversized unisex robe."

**Under Armour connection (founder's claim on Shark Tank, per Gazette Review):** Busch says he was involved in the beginnings of Under Armour, negotiating contracts from his first year of law school, and declined equity at the time. This is his own account as reported; treat as founder-claimed, not independently verified.

### DUDE Wipes connection — VERIFIED: none

DudeRobe and DUDE Products (Dude Wipes) are **separate companies with no shared founders**:
- DudeRobe: Howie Busch, Long Island NY, founded 2017, Kickstarter.
- Dude Wipes: Sean Riley, Ryan Meegan, Jeff Klimkowski and Brian Wilkin, Chicago, founded 2011/2012, Shark Tank Season 7 (2015), $300,000 from Mark Cuban for 25%.
- No source other than one low-quality page claims any link. That page (sharktanksuccess.com, "DUDE Products Update") states "The company also branched out into clothing. They created the DudeRobe" — this contradicts the Kickstarter page (creator: Howie Busch), the PRWeb release, Inc., Boardroom, and all Shark Tank coverage. Treat it as an error. DUDE Products' own product range is wipes-led; no robe product of theirs was found.
- The shared "Dude" naming and the shared Shark Tank history are the only links, and both are coincidence, not corporate connection. Do **not** use Dude Wipes' story (Chicago apartment, Mark Cuban deal, $300M valuation) as DudeRobe material.
