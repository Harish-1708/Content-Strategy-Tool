## 1. Origin / founder

**Founder:** Tatyana Jones — Ukrainian immigrant, former CPG executive, CrossFit enthusiast, MBA from NYU Stern.

**Corporate background (before founding):** Johnson & Johnson, Mars Wrigley (where she worked on Dove Chocolate), GSK Consumer, Bausch + Lomb.
- Source: Adventures Frugal Mom, "Top 10 Entrepreneurs Transforming Industries", published 2026-04-22 — "Before launching DEFI Snacks in November 2024, Tatyana built her impressive career working for big names like Johnson & Johnson, Mars Wrigley, GSK Consumer and Bausch + Lomb. This unique blend of corporate experience and education from NYU Stern School of Business helped Tatyana spot what was missing in the market."
- URL: https://adventuresfrugalmom.com/top-10-entrepreneurs-transforming-industries/
- Corroborated by Tapesearch ("Oh, My Health… There Is HOPE!" episode notes): "Tatyana has previously helped grow well-known brands such as Dove Chocolate and Mars Wrigley. An MBA graduate from NYU Stern…" — https://www.tapesearch.com/podcast/oh-my-health-there-is-hope/1477858454

**The origin story (brand's own account, About page):**
- "DEFI started in Tatyana's kitchen. Growing up Ukrainian, buckwheat was a staple — and when she moved to the US, she couldn't believe a superfood this good was practically unknown. (For the record: it's not a grain or a wheat. It's a flower seed — closer to a fruit.)"
- "The shelves were full of better-for-you snacks and full of indulgent ones — and nothing that was honestly both. So she started experimenting at home, then brought in a chef to develop many of the flavors you see today: real chocolate, sprouted buckwheat crunch, no compromises."
- "She didn't sell it at first. She brought batches to the gym and gave them away — and people kept coming back for more. Then a retailer placed a sizable order before ever tasting the product. That's when it was clear: DEFI wasn't just a snack. It was a brand."
- Source: DEFI Snacks About page — https://www.defi-snacks.com/pages/about (read 2026-11-16)

**Founder's own words (About page):**
- "As a fitness-loving foodie, I launched DEFI to revolutionize snacking. By choosing DEFI, you're not just enjoying a delicious snack – you're supporting a female-led brand that proudly donates a portion of its sales to women-run businesses that defy the odds, just like us."
- Source: https://www.defi-snacks.com/pages/about

**Why buckwheat (founder quote, press release):**
- "Most people assume buckwheat is a wheat or some type of a grain," says Tatyana. "In reality, it's neither. Buckwheat is a naturally gluten-free flower seed, and nutritionally outperforms many of today's trendy ingredients. It's great for your gut, is loaded with fiber, protein, vitamins and antioxidants… and the list goes on."
- Source: CPG Media / BevNET newswire, 2026-01-13 — https://www.cpgmedia.com/newswire/2026/01/13/redefining-betterforyou-snacking-defi-snacks-combines-superfood-innovation-with-timereleased-fuel

**How the product was built:**
- After moving to the US and spotting the gap, "Tatyana partnered with a trusted scientist to explore white-space opportunities for a buckwheat-based product. After much experimentation and category analysis, she identified a clear gap in the market: snacks that are truly indulgent, yet go beyond empty calories with nutrient density and function."
- "Shortly after, Tatyana partnered with Chef Jean-François Bonnet, known for helping formulate many of the original Hu Chocolate flavors before the brand was sold to snacking giant, Mondelez."
- Source: CPG Media newswire, 2026-01-13 (same URL as above)
- Corroborated by Expo West 2026 exhibitor listing: "As a passionate Ukrainian immigrant with a love for fitness and wholesome food, I founded DEFI Snacks to share my heritage and vision with the world. Growing up in Ukraine, I cherished buckwheat — a nutrient-rich, gluten-free staple… I dreamed of creating a snack that married my cultural roots with my active lifestyle as a dedicated CrossFitter." — https://attend.expowest.com/event/natural-products-expo-west-2026/exhibitor/RXhoaWJpdG9yXzIzNTg5NDg=

**Launch:** November 2024 (product launch; see Milestones).
- Source: Adventures Frugal Mom, 2026-04-22 (same URL as above)

**Mission / giving (wording varies by source — keep source wording):**
- About page: "With every bag sold, we donate 1% of sales to women-owned businesses. Less than 2% of venture funding goes to female founders. We're here to change that. DEFI is a proud partner of the Enthuse Foundation, a nonprofit dedicated to empowering women entrepreneurs to grow from new to next."
- Press release (2026-01-13): "As a female-founded company, DEFI donates 1% of its profitable sales to women-owned startups that defy the odds."
- Source: https://www.defi-snacks.com/pages/about and https://www.cpgmedia.com/newswire/2026/01/13/redefining-betterforyou-snacking-defi-snacks-combines-superfood-innovation-with-timereleased-fuel

**Product (for context, from the Jan 2026 press release):** four crispy chocolate bites — Dark Chocolate Himalayan Salt, Dark Chocolate, Milk Chocolate, Milk Chocolate Peanut Butter Crunch (new). 25–26g protein per bag. Built on sprouted organic buckwheat + whey protein crisps (fast energy) + European-grade casein (slow digestion, longer fullness). No sugar substitutes.
- Source: CPG Media newswire, 2026-01-13 (same URL as above)
